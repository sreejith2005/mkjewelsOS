# Ask Kiara: announcement for employees

For the owner to send. Part A is ready to paste into WhatsApp (English, then Hinglish).
Part B is a one-page guide in simple Hinglish that can be printed or shared as a picture.
Before sending, check the daily limit on Ask Kiara → **Limits** (default 10) and change the
number below if you changed it.

---

## Part A. WhatsApp announcement

### English

> **Meet Kiara, our new helper in JewelOS** ✨
>
> Kiara answers your work questions in seconds: your tasks, our company SOPs, and how to use JewelOS.
>
> **How to open it:** open JewelOS on the web (https://mkjewels-os.vercel.app) and tap **Ask Kiara** in the menu. It will come to the JewelOS phone app in the next update.
>
> **Try asking:**
> 1. "What is the process for old gold purchase?" (company SOP)
> 2. "What is pending for me today?" (your tasks)
> 3. "How do I apply for leave?" (using JewelOS)
>
> You can type in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Managers can also use the mic.
>
> **Good to know:**
> - You can ask **10 questions a day**. The count resets at midnight.
> - Kiara only uses what **you can already see** in JewelOS. It cannot see anyone else's private information.
> - Kiara only answers. It does not do any work for you (it will not complete a task or approve leave).
> - If Kiara can't find the answer, press **Ask a person**. Your question goes to your manager, and the answer comes back in the same chat.
> - SOP answers show a **source**. Tap it to read the SOP yourself.

## Part B. Kiara kaise use karein (ek page)

**Kiara kya hai?** JewelOS ke andar ek helper. Aap sawaal likhiye, Kiara jawab degi: aapke kaam,
company ke SOP, aur JewelOS ke buttons ke baare mein.

**Kholna**
1. Phone ya computer par Chrome mein **https://mkjewels-os.vercel.app** kholiye aur login kariye.
2. Menu mein **Ask Kiara** dabaiye.

**Sawaal poochna**
3. Neeche wale box mein apna sawaal likhiye. English, Hindi ya Hinglish, sab chalega.
4. **Send** (teer wala button) dabaiye. Kuch second mein jawab aayega.
5. Mic dikhe to mic dabakar bol bhi sakte hain. Bolne ke baad stop dabaiye, likhe hue shabd check kariye, phir **Send**.

**Jawab samajhna**
6. SOP wale jawab ke neeche **Sources** mein ek chip hota hai. Use dabaiye, poora SOP hissa khul jayega.
7. Upar ka counter batata hai aaj kitne sawaal bache hain (roz **10**). Raat 12 baje phir se 10.

**Jab jawab na mile**
8. Kiara bolegi ki SOP mein nahi mila, aur **Ask a person** button dikhayegi.
9. Button dabaiye. Sawaal aapke manager ke paas chala jayega. Jawab aane par notification aayega, aur jawab usi chat mein dikhega.

**Nayi baat shuru karna**
10. **New chat** dabaiye. Purani chats list mein rehti hain (phone par **Chats** button mein).

**Yaad rakhiye**
- Kiara sirf wahi dikhati hai jo aap khud JewelOS mein dekh sakte hain.
- Kiara koi kaam nahi karti: task complete, leave approve, ya kuch delete nahi karti. Sirf batati hai kaise karna hai.
- Customer ka poora phone number ya address Kiara nahi batati. Sirf aakhri 4 number aur CRM ka link.
- Galat jawab lage to apne manager ko bataiye: sawaal aur time likh kar.

**Achhe sawaal ke udaharan**
- "Aaj mera kya pending hai?"
- "Mere overdue tasks kaun se hain?"
- "Showroom kholne ka SOP kya hai?"
- "Form kaise bharte hain?"
- "Kal kaun leave par hai?" (managers)
